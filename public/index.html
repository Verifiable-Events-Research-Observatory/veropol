    Sources & Methodology

    How information reaches VERO

    VERO does not write news. It reads the public RSS feeds that vetted outlets publish in several languages and adds results from a licensed news aggregation interface. Only outlets on the vetted list can appear, and reports are shown in their original language first, then machine-translated into the reader's language on request.

    How outlets are chosen

    VERO selects established outlets with a public editorial process, a large audience and a track record of issuing corrections. Perspectives are deliberately mixed: Arab regional media, European public and independent media, United Nations reporting, and US-based wire services and non-profit broadcasters.

    No outlet is perfectly neutral, and VERO does not claim otherwise. Some outlets on the list are funded or owned by states or state-linked groups. They are included for regional perspective, and the region of each outlet is shown so that readers can weigh its viewpoint.

    When the vetted sources hold little on a topic, Explore adds reports from the wider web. These are labeled Web and are not part of the vetted list.

    How results are compared and ranked

    Results are ranked by how closely a headline and description match the search, how relevant they are to geopolitics, and how recent they are. Duplicate headlines are removed. Different reports are never merged, rewritten or summarized into one, so differences between outlets stay visible.

    What VERO does not do

    VERO does not copy full articles, bypass paywalls, use anonymous blogs or social media posts as sources, or sell placement. Every card links to the original publisher.

    Translation

    All site text, search terms and news cards are translated by machine using Google Cloud Translation. Machine translation can contain errors, especially for names and idioms. Please report mistakes through the feedback form.

      Privacy Policy

      Scope

      This policy describes how VERO processes personal data when the site is visited, searched, used to send feedback, or used through an account.

      Visitors

      VERO uses Google Analytics to measure anonymous, aggregate usage such as pages viewed, device type and approximate region. Cookies may be set for this purpose. The hosting provider processes IP addresses in server logs as part of normal operation. Personal data is never sold.

      Search and translation

      Search terms are sent to Google Cloud Translation and to the news provider to return relevant reports. Searches are not stored in the VERO database. Recent searches, theme and language choices remain in the visitor's own browser and can be cleared at any time.

      Accounts

      Accounts are available to persons aged 13 or older. VERO stores a username, email address, password (as a salted hash), name, date of birth, and an optional photo and short bio. The username, name, photo, bio, badges, articles, comments and follower counts are public. The email address and date of birth are never displayed. The date of birth is used only to apply age requirements. Accounts under 18 may read, react, comment and follow, but may not publish articles.

      Activity and notifications

      Comments, reactions and follows are stored to operate the service. In-app notifications can be switched off, in whole or by type, in the account settings. Browser alerts are shown only with the user's permission.

      Feedback

      Feedback messages are stored together with the name and email address, if provided, so that a reply is possible. Both are optional.

      Retention and rights

      An account and all associated content can be deleted at any time in the account settings. Access, correction or deletion of personal data can also be requested by email to veropol@proton.me.

      Security

      Passwords are hashed, traffic is encrypted, and administrative access is restricted.
