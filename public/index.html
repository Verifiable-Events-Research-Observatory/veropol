Sources & Methodology

How information reaches VERO

VERO does not write news. We read the public RSS feeds that vetted outlets publish in several languages, and we add results from a licensed news aggregation interface. Only outlets on our vetted list can appear, and articles are shown in their original language first, then machine-translated into yours on request.

How outlets are chosen

We select established outlets with a public editorial process, a large audience and a track record of issuing corrections. We deliberately mix perspectives: Arab regional media, European public and independent media, United Nations reporting, and US-based wire services and non-profit broadcasters.

No outlet is perfectly neutral, and we do not claim otherwise. Some outlets on the list are funded or owned by states or state-linked groups. We include them for regional perspective and show the region of each outlet so you can weigh its viewpoint.

How we compare and rank

Results are ranked by how closely a headline and description match your search, how relevant they are to geopolitics, and how recent they are. Duplicate headlines are removed. We never merge, rewrite or summarize different reports into one, so differences between outlets stay visible.

What we do not do

We do not copy full articles, bypass paywalls, use anonymous blogs or social media posts as sources, or sell placement. Every card links to the original publisher.

Translation

All site text, search terms and news cards are translated by machine using Google Cloud Translation. Machine translation can contain errors, especially for names and idioms. Please report mistakes through the feedback form.

    Privacy Policy

    Visiting the site

    We use Google Analytics to measure anonymous, aggregate usage such as pages viewed, device type and approximate region. Google Analytics may set cookies. Our hosting provider also processes IP addresses in server logs as part of normal operation. We do not sell personal data.

    Searches and translation

    The words you search for are sent to Google Cloud Translation to detect their language and to translate them, and to our news provider to retrieve headlines. We do not store your searches in our database. Recent searches, theme and language choices stay in your own browser and can be cleared at any time.

    Feedback

    If you send feedback, we store your message and, if you provide them, your name and email so that we can reply. Both are optional.

    Article contributors

    To write articles you create an account with your email, a password, your name, age (18 or older), nationality and country of residence. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. A session cookie keeps you signed in. A profile photo is required and an article cover is optional. Publicly, only your first name, the initial of your last name, your nationality, your photo and a verification badge appear. Your age, email and residence are never shown. We never ask for copies of identity documents, and you can delete your account and all of your articles at any time.

    Your rights

    You can ask to access, correct or delete your data at any time by emailing veropol@proton.me.
